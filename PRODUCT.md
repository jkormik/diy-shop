# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Fans of handmade crafts who value uniqueness and slow-craft. They typically browse from cozy environments, seeking unique gifts for themselves or loved ones to decorate their homes.

## Product Purpose
To provide a curated marketplace for unique, handmade embroidery and decor that brings warmth and personality into a living space.

## Positioning
Driven by the "inner fire" and passion of its creator—a tough, passionate woman whose energy and commitment to the craft are embedded in every piece.

## Operating Context
A slow-commerce experience where the value lies in the time and passion invested in each item, contrasting with mass-produced home decor.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Handmade & Unique**: Every item is created manually and uniquely.
- **Slow Craft**: Work is intentionally made "not in a hurry," prioritizing quality and soul over speed of production.
- **Core Flow**: Category browsing $\to$ Product discovery $\to$ Favoriting/Carting $\to$ Purchase.

## Brand Commitments
- **Voice**: Passionate, soulful, and authentic.
- **Identity**: Reflects the strength and passion of the creator while maintaining the "cozy" appeal of the products.

## Product Principles
1. **Soul over Speed**: Honor the "slow craft" nature; the interface should reflect quality and intention, not urgency.
2. **Passionate Strength**: Balance the cozy, soft nature of embroidery with the tough, passionate energy of the creator.
3. **Uncompromising Uniqueness**: Highlight the "one-of-a-kind" nature of every item.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG AA compliance.
